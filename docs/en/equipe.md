# The team

The **Blades Runners 92** are a FIRST Tech Challenge team from Levallois-Perret, just outside Paris (Hauts-de-Seine, France), **hosted by Lycée Léonard de Vinci**. Young people, students at the school or from elsewhere, who design, build, program and drive a competition robot, supported by coaches.

<div class="br-stats">
  <div><strong>1</strong><span>robot per season, designed from scratch</span></div>
  <div><strong>4</strong><span>groups, and everyone does a bit of everything</span></div>
  <div><strong>2:30</strong><span>the length of a match</span></div>
  <div><strong>100%</strong><span>of what we learn, shared</span></div>
</div>

## Our groups

<div class="br-cards" markdown>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-cog-outline:
### Mechanical and CAD
<p>We model the robot in 3D, prototype, machine and assemble. Chassis, arms, grippers: everything starts as a sketch and ends up on the field.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-code-braces:
### Software
<p>Java and the FTC SDK: driver control, autonomous period, sensors, vision. Everything goes through GitHub, with code review.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-lightning-bolt-outline:
### Electrical
<p>Control Hub, motors, servos, sensors: clean, reliable wiring, because a loose connector is a lost match.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-bullhorn-outline:
### Communication and outreach
<p>Engineering portfolio, social media, finding partners, and this knowledge base.</p>
</div>
</div>

## How we work

<div class="br-split" markdown>

<div markdown>

**Gracious Professionalism.** In FTC, we play to win, but never against others. Helping an opposing team fix their robot between matches is just normal.

**Coopertition.** Every match, we form alliances with teams we will face in the next one. Knowing how to cooperate matters as much as knowing how to design.

</div>

<div markdown>

**Document.** Every design decision goes into the engineering portfolio: why we chose it, what we tried, what failed.

**Share.** What we learn, we publish in the [knowledge base](kb/index.md), in French and English, so the next teams start further ahead.

</div>

</div>

## An FTC season

<div class="br-timeline">
  <div><strong>September · Kickoff</strong><p>FIRST reveals the season's game: field, game elements, rules. Everyone discovers it on the same day.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Fall · Design</strong><p>Rules analysis, strategy, sketches, prototypes, CAD. The robot takes shape, and so does the code.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Winter · Qualifiers</strong><p>First competitions: alliance matches, judging interviews, portfolio presentation.</p></div>
  <div><strong>Spring · Championships</strong><p>Qualified teams move on to the national championship, and the best ones to the world championship.</p></div>
</div>
