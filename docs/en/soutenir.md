# Support us

An FTC season means a robot to fund, competition registrations, travel, and a lot to learn. Your support makes all of that possible, and lets us keep sharing what we learn for free.

## How you can help

<div class="br-cards" markdown>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-hand-coin-outline:
### Sponsorship
<p>Financial support for the season's parts, registration and travel.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-toolbox-outline:
### Equipment and services
<p>Machining, 3D printing, cutting, tools, components: anything that moves the robot forward.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-account-tie-outline:
### Mentoring
<p>Engineers, developers, designers: an hour of your expertise is worth weeks of trial and error.</p>
</div>
<div class="br-card" markdown>:material-share-variant-outline:
### Visibility
<p>Talk about us, share the knowledge base, invite us to present the project.</p>
</div>
</div>

## What's in it for you

- **Your logo** on the robot, on this site and on our competition materials.
- **A direct link with young people** learning engineering, coding and teamwork in real conditions.
- **A project with reach** beyond the team: our guides are used by French-speaking FTC teams across the community.
